## Accepted / noted (non-blocking, no change)

- The new `500` fallback on `/wake` and `/webhook/telegram` is not enumerated in `contracts/openapi.yaml`
  (which lists 200 + 401). A defensive 5xx for unexpected internal errors is a standard, universally
  understood fallback and does not diverge from the designed happy/auth paths — accepted as-is.
- AC-06's "favor not delivering again" preference in the genuinely-ambiguous send-succeeded-then-crashed
  window is **narrowed, not closed**, by the graceful-shutdown drain — the accepted at-least-once
  trade-off from ADR-0005, as documented in spec §1 and now accurately in `data-model.md`.
